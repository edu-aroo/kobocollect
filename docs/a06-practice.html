Exercise 3 — Analyse in RExercice 3 — Analyser dans R

Build three tables as in Session A5: fishers (p_index), trips (s_index, p_index), catches (s_index → p_index). Question 6: compute the weight per trip first (sum of its catches), then the mean per gear. Question 8: fishers whose p_index is not among the p_index of the octopus catches.

Construisez trois tables comme à la séance A5 : pêcheurs (p_index), sorties (s_index, p_index), captures (s_index → p_index). Question 6 : calculez d’abord le poids par sortie (somme de ses captures), puis la moyenne par engin. Question 8 : pêcheurs dont le p_index n’est pas parmi les p_index des captures de poulpe.

1 75 · 2 141 · 3 592.1 · 4 2.5 · 5 Milenaka · 6 fusil · 7 53.3 % · 8 8 · 9 poisson_recif · 10 51.8 kg.

1 75 · 2 141 · 3 592.1 · 4 2.5 · 5 Milenaka · 6 fusil · 7 53.3 % · 8 8 · 9 poisson_recif · 10 51.8 kg.

source("kobo_import.R")
k  <- read_kobo("a06_pecheurs_xml.xlsx")
m  <- k$main |> select(p_index = `_index`, commune, pecheur)
so <- k$sorties  |> rename(s_index = `_index`, p_index = `_parent_index`) |> left_join(m, by = "p_index")
ca <- k$captures |> rename(s_index = `_parent_index`) |>
      left_join(so |> select(s_index, p_index, engin, commune), by = "s_index")
nrow(so); nrow(ca); sum(ca$poids); nrow(so) / nrow(m)                         # 1-4
ca |> group_by(commune) |> summarise(w = sum(poids)) |> arrange(desc(w))      # 5 (then kobo_label)
so |> left_join(ca |> group_by(s_index) |> summarise(w = sum(poids)), by = "s_index") |>
  group_by(engin) |> summarise(m = mean(w, na.rm = TRUE)) |> arrange(desc(m)) # 6
mean(so$s_index %in% ca$s_index[ca$espece == "poulpe"]) * 100                 # 7
sum(!m$p_index %in% ca$p_index[ca$espece == "poulpe"])                        # 8
ca |> count(espece, wt = poids, sort = TRUE)                                  # 9
ca |> group_by(p_index) |> summarise(w = sum(poids)) |> slice_max(w)          # 10
